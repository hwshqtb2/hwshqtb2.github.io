This page is the chapter root of the **Guides** category. Creating a page with
`categories: [guides]` and `order: 0` turns it into the group root:

- every ordered guide links back here ("Back to category") and gets
  previous/next links;
- this page shows the generated table of contents below.

The license in the front matter is inherited by the other `article` pages in the
category (see `_plugins/DefaultLicense.rb`), so each guide shows the same
license in the footer without repeating it.

Ordered guides in this demo: `Getting started` (order 1) and `Advanced usage`
(order 2).
