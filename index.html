--- layout: home title: "Jekyll Authors Template" ---

A multi-author blog and wiki starter for Jekyll. Every author writes in their own folder under authors-src/; the plugins in _plugins/ merge those folders into the site at build time.

Pages can join a category by adding categories: to their front matter. Category pages list their members automatically, ordered pages get previous/next navigation and a table of contents, and tags, feeds and a "Recently updated" sidebar are generated for you.
