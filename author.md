Every author profile is a normal page with `categories: [author]`, written as
`authors-src/<author>/author.md`. During the build it is mirrored to
`author/<title>.html`.

The list below is generated automatically from the `author/` directory. Remove
the demo authors in `authors-src/` and replace them with your own.
